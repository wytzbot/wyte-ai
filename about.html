About WYTE AI

wyte Tech Company (WTC)

WYTE AI is an AI sales assistant for businesses that sell through WhatsApp. It's built by wyte Tech Company (WTC), founded by Ilemobayo Tolulope.

The problem WYTE AI exists to solve is simple: customers message on WhatsApp all day, and no vendor — however dedicated — can answer everyone instantly. WYTE AI connects to a vendor's existing WhatsApp Business catalog and answers the repetitive questions (price, size, availability, delivery) using the vendor's real product information, while handing off to the vendor whenever a customer needs a person.

What we don't do

We don't invent product information, and we don't claim guaranteed sales or business outcomes. WYTE AI is a tool to help a vendor respond faster and more consistently — the vendor remains responsible for their products, prices, orders and customer relationships.
