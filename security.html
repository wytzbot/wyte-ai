Security

We take reasonable, concrete steps to protect vendor and customer data. We don't claim to be "100% secure" or hold certifications we don't have — here's plainly what's actually in place:

  • Firebase Authentication for vendor accounts, with all dashboard routes requiring sign-in
  • Firestore Security Rules that isolate each vendor's data from every other vendor's, enforced server-side, not just hidden in the UI
  • Premium status, payments, and AI usage limits are enforced and writable only by our backend — never trusted from the browser
  • Payment webhooks are signature-verified and the underlying transaction is re-verified directly with Flutterwave before Premium is activated, with duplicate-webhook protection
  • WhatsApp webhook messages are signature-verified against Meta's app secret before being processed
  • Secrets (API keys, access tokens) are stored as environment variables on our hosting provider and are never sent to the browser
  • Errors shown to customers and vendors are generic and safe; technical details are logged server-side only, not exposed

If you discover a security issue, please contact us — see Contact.
