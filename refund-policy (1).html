Refund Policy

Premium payments

Premium is billed monthly and manually (not auto-renewed). If you believe you were charged in error, or a payment didn't activate Premium correctly, contact us within 7 days of the charge.

How to request a refund

Email us at the address on our Contact page with your transaction reference (shown on your payment receipt email and in Billing). We'll review the request and respond with a decision.

Eligibility

Refunds are considered case by case — for example, a duplicate charge, or a payment that didn't activate the plan it was for. We don't offer automatic refunds for simply changing your mind after Premium has been active and used.

Processing

Approved refunds are processed back through Flutterwave to your original payment method. Processing times depend on your bank or payment provider.
