Frequently asked questions

What is WYTE AI?

An AI sales assistant that connects to your WhatsApp Business account and answers customers using your real product catalog.

Does it replace me?

No. It handles repetitive questions and order collection; you stay in control and can pause it any time.

Can customers order while I'm busy?

Yes — the assistant can collect order details any time, including outside your business hours, and summarizes them for your confirmation flow.

Does it use my WhatsApp catalog?

Yes. You connect your WhatsApp Business catalog and WYTE AI syncs product information from there.

Do I need Firebase Storage or to re-upload my products?

No. Product images stay referenced from your catalog; WYTE AI doesn't store a second copy.

Can I pause the AI and speak to customers myself?

Yes, any time, from your dashboard.

What happens when the AI doesn't know something?

It says so honestly and offers to connect the customer with you, rather than guessing.

How do I pay, and how do I renew?

Payments go through Flutterwave. Premium is currently manual renewal, not automatic — see Pricing.

Is my data safe?

See our Security and Privacy pages.

What happens if WhatsApp or the AI provider is unavailable?

The assistant falls back to a direct, honest response rather than going silent, and never invents information during an outage.
