AI Disclosure

WYTE AI uses artificial intelligence to help vendors have conversations with their customers on WhatsApp — answering questions, recommending products, and collecting order details.

How it stays grounded

The assistant is instructed to use only information a vendor has actually provided: their catalog, business settings, and FAQs. It's designed not to invent prices, stock, sizes, colours, discounts, delivery fees, or policies. When it doesn't have an answer, it's designed to say so and offer to connect the customer with the vendor, rather than guess.

It can still make mistakes

Like any AI system, it can misunderstand a message or respond imperfectly. Vendors remain responsible for their product information, prices, inventory, orders, delivery, refunds and customer service.

Human control

Vendors can pause the assistant at any time and take over conversations directly.
