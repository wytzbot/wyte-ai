FAQ

Common questions

Does it replace me?

No. It handles repetitive questions and order collection. You stay in control, can pause it any time, and it hands off to you when a customer needs a person.

Does it use my existing WhatsApp catalog?

Yes — that's the intended setup. You connect your WhatsApp Business catalog and WYTE AI syncs the product information from there, rather than asking you to re-enter everything.

What happens when it doesn't know something?

It says so, and offers to connect the customer with you. It's built not to invent prices, stock, or policies.

Can I speak to customers myself?

Yes, any time. Pause the assistant from your dashboard whenever you want to take over a conversation.
