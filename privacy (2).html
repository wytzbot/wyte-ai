Privacy Policy

This is a general-purpose draft — have it reviewed for your jurisdiction before launch.

What we collect

  • Vendor account data: business details, email, phone, settings you configure
  • Customer data from WhatsApp conversations: phone number, name if given, conversation history bounded to recent turns, order details
  • Catalog data synced from your connected WhatsApp Business catalog
  • Payment records from Flutterwave (we do not store card numbers — Flutterwave processes payments directly)

How we use it

To operate the AI assistant, sync your catalog, process orders and payments, and show you analytics about your own business. Customer conversation data is used only to serve that customer and is not shared with other vendors.

Third parties

We use Firebase (Google) for authentication and data storage, Vercel for hosting, WhatsApp/Meta for messaging and catalog access, Flutterwave for payments, an AI provider for conversational responses, and optionally Google Sheets if you connect it. Each has its own privacy practices.

Data retention and deletion

Conversation history is kept in bounded, recent form rather than indefinitely. You can request account deletion — see our Disclaimer and Settings for details; some records may be retained where required for financial or legal purposes.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by contacting us — see Contact.
